# Changelog

## 1.3.0

The quality-of-life release: wood variants, gates that shut themselves on an enemy, and a hole in
the permission system closed.

### Added

- **Eleven woods** for the Medieval Gate, the Drawbridge and the Bascule Drawbridge: oak, spruce,
  birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak, mangrove, cherry, bamboo, crimson and warped. Each has its own
  artwork at every one of the eleven gate sizes and in every wear stage, and is crafted from its own
  logs. Balance is identical across woods on purpose.
- **Gates that shut themselves.** An enemy walking up to an open gate closes it. Configured under
  `siege` in the server config, and off the moment `autoClose` is false.
- **`/gate enemy add|remove|list`** — your own list of who you are at war with, applying to every
  gate you own rather than to one doorway at a time.
- **Claim mod integration.** FTB Chunks (with FTB Teams for alliances) and Open Parties and Claims
  are read through their own APIs; everything else that protects blocks is covered by a generic
  probe. None of it is a dependency. Allies of a gate's owner can open it without being trusted
  individually, which `siege.trustClaimAllies` turns off.
- `/gate info` now reports the gate's footprint.
- Defenders nearby are told when one of their gates shuts itself on somebody
  (`siege.warnDefenders`).

### Changed

- **Operators only override a gate's access list in creative mode.** Permission level alone used to
  be enough, which made a moderator playing the server's own survival world a skeleton key: every
  locked gate on the map opened to them, and nothing told the owner. The rule covers the `/gate`
  commands as well as opening the gate, because an operator who could still run `/gate op` in
  survival would simply grant themselves the gate. `general.adminBypassRequiresCreative` restores
  the old behaviour.
- **The oak gate and bridges now need oak logs**, not any log. Each wood is crafted from its own,
  the way vanilla doors are.
- The gate's footprint moved from the blockstate onto the block entity. No visible effect, and the
  reason is arithmetic: eleven footprints times eleven woods would have come to roughly 1.9 million
  blockstates, each of which Minecraft builds a shape cache for at startup. The mod now registers
  about 169,000 — fewer than version 1.2 did with four gates and one wood — and builds no caches at
  all, because its shapes are declared dynamic.
- Gates saved by an older version are measured from their own parts the first time they load, so
  existing castles keep their owners, their access lists and their shape. A gate whose parts no
  longer add up to a valid footprint is taken apart into loose blocks and logged, rather than left
  standing as the wrong size.
- Wear artwork for the oak gate and the decks now stops at stage 3, which is as far as the new
  artwork goes; a fifth stage, if configured, reuses it rather than showing the old mismatched
  texture.
- The mod is **open source under the MIT licence**, and the per-wood resources are generated by
  `tools/generate_resources.py` rather than written by hand.

### Fixed

- A gate could be opened by an operator in survival mode even while locked.

## 1.2 and earlier

Not tracked here. Four gate types, eleven footprints, staged damage, siege-mod compatibility,
ownership and trust, per-size textures and models, and the drawbridge's pathfinding support.
